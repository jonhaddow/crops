# Roadmap

Ideas for after the prototype, roughly in order:

- Sticker book: every crop and animal collected so far.
- Going inside houses.
- More animals (cow and milk, sheep and wool), and naming the horse.
- Seasons, with crops that only grow in some.
- A second field, bought with coins.
- Villagers with gentle requests ("3 carrots for Bunny").
- Sound on/off button.
